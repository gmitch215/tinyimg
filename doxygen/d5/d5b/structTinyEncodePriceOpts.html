Detailed Description

What an encode request carries beyond its extent, for pricing.

A zeroed structure prices the defaults, which is what tiny_encode_cost_at prices. Each field is a lever the encoder reads and the price follows:

  • quality for JPEG, WebP and AVIF, at the nearest of the measured points 50, 75, 85, 90 and 95; zero is the format's own default.
  • lossless for WebP, the only format here with a lossless mode measured.
  • compression for PNG and deflate-compressed TIFF.
  • channels, the source's channel count; 2 and 4 carry an alpha channel, and every other value prices as opaque.
  • effort, a TinyEffort for the encoder; PNG and lossy WebP read it.

The factors were measured one at a time against the default. A request that moves several at once multiplies them, and that product is not itself measured.
