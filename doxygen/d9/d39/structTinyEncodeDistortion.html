Detailed Description

What an output format costs in bytes at one quality, and what it gives back.

Measured at 400 px wide on candidate 0's pixels of opaque images. The PSNR is against the raw pixels after an external decoder, never tinyimg's own. For PNG it is 99 and the class is EXACT; for GIF it is the palette's PSNR, which does not depend on quality.
