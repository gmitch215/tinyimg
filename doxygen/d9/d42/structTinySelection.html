Detailed Description

What the search decided and everything it looked at.

Read it through tiny_selection_field and tiny_selection_considered_field; the layout is not part of the ABI. It is about 7 KB, so keep one on the stack only where the stack allows.

What the predicted figures are. The dB is the PSNR against the plan's own undegraded output (candidate 0), composed from two table lookups by tiny_select_compose_db: the lever's fifth percentile and the encoder's fifth percentile. Composing percentiles of two independent distributions is not the fifth percentile of their sum; it is conservative if both rarely fall low together. The measured coverage of each part's own p05 on images that were not in the fit is carried in the selection, because it is under the 95 percent the percentile names: 89.3 percent for the lever table overall and 64.5 percent for WebP sources, 94.8 percent for the encode table. The coverage of the composition was not measured.

What the bytes are. The median bytes per output pixel of the output format at its tier, over 99 opaque images at 400 px wide. Image-to-image spread is large, so only the order of two formats at the same tier is relied on; see TECHNICAL_REPORT.md for the measured ordering accuracy. The table was measured on the undegraded pixels, so a candidate with a coarser decode encodes to fewer bytes than predicted.

Alpha. A source with an alpha channel is priced from tables fitted on opaque images, and the lever table's raw PSNR counts RGB under alpha 0, so the dB of an alpha source is pessimistic and its bytes omit the alpha plane. alpha says so.
