◆ assume_opaque

uint32_t TinySelectOpts::assume_opaque

Non-zero when the caller knows the pixels are all opaque even though the source has an alpha channel. The planner reads channel counts, not pixels, so an opaque RGBA source is otherwise treated as having alpha and cannot go to JPEG, AVIF or GIF.

◆ budget_us

uint32_t TinySelectOpts::budget_us

CPU the request may spend in microseconds, on the machine it runs on; zero means no limit, as it does for tiny_plan_set_budget.

◆ formats

uint32_t TinySelectOpts::formats

A bitmask of TINYIMG_SELECT_FORMAT bits for the output formats the search may use. Zero reads as the source's own format (when it is one of the writable ones), JPEG and WebP.

◆ min_db

float TinySelectOpts::min_db

Quality floor, as predicted PSNR in dB against the plan's undegraded output. Zero or negative means no floor. It is compared with the composed fifth percentile (see TinySelection), so it is a prediction that held for 89.3 percent of lever cells and 94.8 percent of encode cells out of sample, not a guarantee.

◆ peak_cap_bytes

uint32_t TinySelectOpts::peak_cap_bytes

Largest peak heap bound a candidate may have. Zero reads as TINYIMG_HEAP_MAX, the heap the module can grow to: the peak is a heap figure, and a single image is separately limited by TINYIMG_MAX_IMAGE_BYTES. The bound covers the plan run and not the encoder.

◆ scale_milli

uint32_t TinySelectOpts::scale_milli

How much slower the running machine is than the one the cost model was calibrated on, times 1000, as in TinyAdmitOpts. Zero reads as 1000.
