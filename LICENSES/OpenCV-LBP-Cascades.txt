The LBP cascades tinyimg embeds are packed from OpenCV's data files by
scripts/cascade.ts. The sources are:

  https://github.com/opencv/opencv/blob/4.x/data/lbpcascades/lbpcascade_frontalface_improved.xml
  https://github.com/opencv/opencv/blob/4.x/data/lbpcascades/lbpcascade_profileface.xml

lbpcascade_frontalface_improved.xml carries this notice, reproduced verbatim
from the file's own header:
