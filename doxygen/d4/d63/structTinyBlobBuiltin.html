Detailed Description

A blob compiled into the module.

The defaults tinyimg ships: one Latin face, four ICC profiles and two LBP cascades, about 45 KB of the module. They need no host wiring, so text drawing, profile conversion and face detection all work off a bare instantiate.

Note
The face and the cascades are third-party data. Their notices are in LICENSES/.
