Detailed Description

What one lever setting costs in quality.

The reference is candidate 0 of tiny_plan_enumerate: the plan's own effort and filter at the decode scale the planner picks undegraded, at the same output extent. Distortion is the PSNR in dB of the lever setting's 8-bit output against the reference's, over every sample of every channel. Two identical outputs are recorded as 99 dB.
